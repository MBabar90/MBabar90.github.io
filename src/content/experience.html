Karachi

Deputy Manager, People Analytics

One of Pakistan's largest groups, with businesses in fertilisers, energy, petrochemicals and telecom infrastructure

I led the modernisation of people analytics reporting, moving the team from manual HR reports to automated, refreshable reporting. Workforce data sat in several SAP HCM instances with no persistent employee ID across them, so I matched records on name, date of birth and national ID in Power Query, building one clean view of well over a million workforce data points ahead of the move to SAP SuccessFactors.

On that foundation, I built HR reporting first in Excel, then in Power BI and Qlik Sense, covering headcount, talent movements and other workforce metrics. This cut the manual effort behind regular reporting by around 70%, made it possible to report more often, and improved data accuracy. The dashboards were used by the CPO, the CFO and heads of department, and I helped standardise KPI definitions so every report told the same story. I also supported workforce planning with forecasts built on the cleaned data.

As reporting moved to the cloud, I supported the AWS team with data mapping and validation between SAP SuccessFactors and the new environment. On an HR chatbot proof of concept, I prepared the data, wrote the test questions and checked its answers against the source data.

I automated work that used to take days, including personalised certificates (PDF and image) and individual emails, each with its own attachments, for 2,500+ employees using Python.
