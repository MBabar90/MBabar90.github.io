Engro · people analytics

One history for every employee

When
2022 – 2026
Role
Led the data reconciliation and migration, and built the reporting
Tools
Power Query, Excel, Power BI, Qlik Sense, SAP HCM, SAP SuccessFactors

Problem

Employees moved between the group's subsidiaries, and each SAP HCM instance issued its own ID. The same person existed as several unconnected records, so no system could show a complete career history.

Illustrative records. Real data stays confidential.

Method

Matched records in Power Query on name, date of birth and national ID. One verified history per employee was loaded into SAP SuccessFactors, and Employee 360 reporting was built on top: first in Excel and Power BI, later in Qlik Sense, and then on the group's AWS platform, where I supported the data team with mapping and validation.

Result

1M+workforce data points in one trusted Employee 360 view
Engro · reporting

Group HR reporting, automated

When
2022 – 2026
Role
Designed and built the automated reporting
Tools
SAP SuccessFactors, Power Query, Power BI, Qlik Sense

Problem

Departments across the subsidiaries received reports assembled by hand from Excel extracts: slow to produce and easy to get wrong. Different reports defined the same KPI in different ways.

Method

Rebuilt the reporting as Power Query transformations feeding Power BI and Qlik Sense, refreshed from SuccessFactors and shaped to each department's needs. Standardised KPI definitions so every report told the same story.

Before
100
After
~30
Manual effort per reporting cycle, indexed to 100. A rough estimate.

Result

~70%less manual effort, with reports delivered more often and more accurately. Used by the CPO, the CFO and heads of department.
SHMA · actuarial

Appointed actuary work for ADNIC

When
2017 – 2022
Role
Analysis and reporting within the SHMA actuarial team
Tools
Excel, Power Query, Alteryx, QlikView

Problem

SHMA was the appointed external actuary for ADNIC, one of the UAE's leading insurers, and had to deliver its Central Bank of UAE actuarial requirements: underwriting reviews, the pricing adequacy report, reserving and ad hoc analysis. The data behind them sat in separate actuarial, claims and reinsurance sources.

Actuarial dataClaims dataReinsurance data
→
Alteryx and Excel framework
→
Pricing adequacyReservingQlik dashboards

Method

Combined the three data sets in integrated Alteryx and Excel frameworks, ran reserving, pricing adequacy and loss variance analysis, and presented the results through Qlik dashboards and variance models.

Result

On scheduleRegulatory deliverables produced on time, with faster reporting cycles and clearer evidence behind pricing, reserving and reinsurance decisions.

ApexPaths · own product

FINSTRIDE, from spreadsheet to app
